# WPS-025 — Manual alpha plan

Status: **NOT RUN**. This plan requires an Android development client and a
representative small-screen device. It is not evidence of execution.

## Required journeys

1. Restart a signed-in worker from customer home and confirm `/worker` opens.
2. Use **Request a service**, confirm customer home opens, restart, and confirm
   worker home returns.
3. Check every dashboard primary state: verification incomplete, under review,
   new request, active job, waiting, unavailable and suspended.
4. Complete the guided journey in Egyptian Arabic and English using large and
   small font settings.
5. Capture National ID front/back and a selfie; replace each image; deny camera
   permission and verify the picker fallback.
6. Upload a valid criminal-record PDF/image and reject unsupported type and
   oversized file cases.
7. Accept a job and execute every status action through completion, including
   running late, evidence, dispute and chat entry.
8. Open earnings at whole-pound, fractional and maximum supported values.
9. Edit the focused profile, including deliberate zero years of experience,
   and verify portfolio/certificate links.
10. Follow worker quote, booking and verification notifications and old deep
    links.
11. Confirm customer home, registration, sign-in and booking remain unchanged.
12. Verify screen-reader labels, RTL order, touch targets and keyboard behavior.

## Screenshot set

Capture worker home in each primary state, every guided-journey step, camera
fallback, verification review, job lifecycle, earnings and focused profile in
Arabic and English. Store no real identity document, phone number, address or
customer information in evidence.
